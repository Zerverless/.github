Zerverless is an AI-native company building the next generation of tooling for
serverless development on AWS. We started from a simple observation: serverless
gives teams incredible leverage, but it also scatters a system across dozens of
managed services, events, functions, and permissions that no one can fully hold
in their head. The result is powerful architecture that's surprisingly hard to
understand, operate, and evolve with confidence.

Our answer is to put AI at the center of the workflow. We use modern models to
turn plain intent into real serverless architecture, to read and explain the
systems already running in your account, and to make cost, security, and
performance legible at a glance. Instead of digging through consoles, logs, and
IAM policies by hand, teams get an intelligent layer that understands AWS deeply
and does the heavy lifting with them.

Everything we build is native to AWS and designed around a single principle:
your workloads stay in your own account. Zerverless provides the intelligence,
visibility, and guidance — never a place to hand over your code. We treat
read-only insight and write access as fundamentally different levels of trust,
and we default to least privilege so adopting our tools never means giving up
control.

We're engineers building for engineers. That means practical tooling over hype,
clarity over complexity, and a strong commitment to open source alongside our
products. Our goal is to make serverless on AWS feel simple, transparent, and
genuinely enjoyable to work with — from the first prompt to production.
